## What Scout Drone is

**Scout Drone** is a mission repo of the Branes.AI platform. Its vehicle flies
to a goal through unknown, static clutter, with onboard sensing only, and is
scored by its success rate against speed. It is built on
[Anatomy](https://branes-ai.github.io/anatomy/), the mission framework, as its
second client after [Racing Drone](https://branes-ai.github.io/racing-drone/).
